# Code of conduct

This project follows the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.
By taking part — issues, pull requests, discussions, events — you agree to it.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good faith, give and take feedback gracefully, and put the people
affected by our software (including the customers whose data it protects) first. Harassment, personal attacks,
discriminatory language and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behaviour to **info@vigyanbytes.com** (subject "Code of conduct"). Reports are handled confidentially
by the maintainers. Enforcement follows the Covenant's community impact guidelines: correction, warning, temporary
ban, permanent ban.
